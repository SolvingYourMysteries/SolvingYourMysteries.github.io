What DNA Can Tell You

DNA can confirm biological relationships with high accuracy. Parent-child, siblings, and even distant relatives can often be identified through shared segments. This makes genetic genealogy a powerful tool in family research and criminal investigations, connecting individuals who may have no documented link on paper.

What DNA Can Tell You

DNA can provide insight into your ancestral origins, but it’s more than a simple percentage. Results are based on comparisons to modern reference populations, reflecting probabilities rather than certainties. As databases grow and methods improve, your “ethnicity estimate” can change over time.

What DNA Can't Tell You

DNA evidence isn’t flawless. Samples can degrade, mixtures can complicate analysis, and interpretation requires human judgment. In forensic cases, DNA might place someone at a scene but doesn’t automatically reveal when or why they were there.
