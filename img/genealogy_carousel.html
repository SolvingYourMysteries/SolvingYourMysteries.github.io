Slide 1 — Misconception

Genealogy misconception
Your ancestor’s records may not be in the country you expect.

One of the biggest misconceptions in genealogy is assuming records will be found in the same country that exists today.

History rarely works that way.

Slide 2 — Borders Moved
Solving Your Mysteries
2
Sometimes your ancestor never moved.
Sometimes the border moved around them.

Borders changed constantly throughout history. Countries split apart, territories were absorbed, counties were renamed, and villages shifted between governments.

Slide 3 — Modern Countries
Solving Your Mysteries
3
Records may now sit in a different modern country.

An ancestor listed as “German” may have records in present-day Poland.

Someone from Austria-Hungary may appear across multiple modern countries.

Someone recorded as Russian may have lived in what is now Lithuania, Ukraine, Poland, or Belarus, depending on when the record was created.

Slide 5 — Search Wider
Solving Your Mysteries
5
Search the place as it was, not just as it is today.

If your ancestors came from regions where borders changed frequently, search across multiple countries’ archives and records collections.

The records you need may not be held where you expect them to be today.

If you only search using modern locations, you can easily miss entire records collections.
