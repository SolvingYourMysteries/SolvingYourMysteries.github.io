Research note · Page not found

404

This page has disappeared from the records.

I checked the census, searched the parish register and asked three elderly relatives. Nothing. It may have changed its name, moved county, or simply never existed.

Possible explanations

Every good mystery needs a few theories.

The link you followed may be old, mistyped or pointing to a page that has moved. In true genealogy fashion, there are several perfectly plausible explanations.

01

It changed its surname. Apparently websites can be just as unhelpful as ancestors.

02

It moved across a boundary. Try explaining that to a search engine using an 1841 county map.

03

The record keeper got creative. One letter out, six letters in. You know how it goes.

04

The link is simply wrong. Less romantic, but considerably more likely.
