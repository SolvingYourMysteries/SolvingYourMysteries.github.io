The “One Record Problem”

A single document can be:

  • Incorrect
  • Incomplete
  • Misread
  • Referring to someone with the same name

Even official records can contain errors.

The “Three Source Rule”

I was once told a simple guideline:

Try to support key facts with at least three independent sources.

It’s not a strict rule, and it’s not always possible, but it’s a strong way to test accuracy.

When You Can’t Get Three Sources

Genealogy isn’t always straightforward.

Sometimes you may only have one or two records available, but that makes careful analysis even more important:

  • Does the timeline make sense?
  • Do names, ages, and locations align?
  • Do related records support the conclusion?
